About This Workshop

Built for Working Professionals

Who this is for

This workshop is designed for IT and cybersecurity professionals with 3–5+ years of hands-on experience who are ready to pursue the CISSP — the Certified Information Systems Security Professional credential issued by ISC².

Whether you are a security analyst, network engineer, IT manager, or systems architect, this structured five-day curriculum takes you through every domain of the CISSP Common Body of Knowledge with the depth and precision the exam demands.

How to use this resource

Work through each lesson in order. Every domain lesson includes a glossary of key terms, a full content outline, scenario-based exam questions, and exam tip callout boxes. Complete Lesson 9 last — the test prep session is most effective once all domains are covered.
