Domain 3 — Applied Practice

The Security Architect's Dilemma

Six high-stakes decisions. Two competing priorities. Think like a manager, not just an engineer.

🧩 6 Branching Scenarios ⏱ 25–35 Minutes 🎯 Full Domain 3 Breadth ⚖️ Posture vs. Continuity

Your Mission

You are the new Security Architect.

The board just hired you to make the structural design choices that determine your organization's security outcomes. Across six real-world situations — spanning secure design, formal models, cryptography, post-quantum readiness, physical security, and cloud — you will make decisions, one stage at a time.

There is rarely a perfect answer. Every choice moves two meters, and the CISSP mindset means knowing when availability and human safety outrank confidentiality.

🛡️ Security PostureRises when you strengthen confidentiality, integrity, and resilience through sound architecture.
⚙️ Business / Safety ContinuityRises when you protect availability, operations, and human life — and falls when you disrupt them needlessly.
🧠 Manager MindsetThe best answer balances both meters. Each choice earns 0, 1, or 2 points toward your final architect rating.
🔁 Learn by consequenceWeak choices aren't dead ends — you'll see what happens, learn the principle, and continue.

This activity assumes you've worked through Lesson 3. It is applied practice — the goal is judgment, not recall.
