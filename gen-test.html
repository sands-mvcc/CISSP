    🚀 How to Use

    1. Configure API: Go to API Config, enter your Anthropic API key, and save.
    2. Select Domain: Choose a specific CBK domain or "All Domains" for a comprehensive 50-question exam.
    3. Focus (Optional): Select a specific topic from the domain, and/or type a concept keyword to target questions further.
    4. Set Quantity: Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 questions (50 for comprehensive).
    5. Generate: Click "Generate CISSP Practice Test" and wait for AI to create your questions.
    6. Take the Test: Read each scenario carefully and select your best answer.
    7. Review: Click "Check Answers" for immediate feedback with full explanations.

    💡 Study Tips

    • Start with the domain you find most challenging — generate multiple sets for deep coverage.
    • Use the Concept Filter to drill into specific models, protocols, or frameworks (e.g., "Bell-LaPadula," "chain of custody," "SAML federation").
    • Read every explanation carefully, even when you answered correctly — the "why wrong" reasoning is CISSP gold.
    • CISSP questions are scenario-based and managerial. Ask yourself: "What would a CISO do?"
    • Generate a 50-question comprehensive exam periodically to simulate the actual adaptive CAT format.
    • Domain 1 (Security & Risk Management) carries the most weight — revisit it often.
    • Always supplement with official (ISC)² study materials and the Official CISSP CBK Reference.
    • Vary your question count: use 5-question focused sets for quick review, 25-question sets for endurance.
