Blue Team Network Defense Lab

Seven attack waves are inbound. Arm the right control, place it in the right zone, and run the simulation.

🛰️ 7 Attack Waves ⏱ 20–30 Minutes 🎯 Full Domain 4 Breadth ⚖️ Effectiveness vs. Health

Your Mission

You are the blue-team network engineer.

The organization's network spans an internet edge, a DMZ, an internal LAN, a data center, a wireless segment, remote workers, and a cloud VPC. Across seven waves, attackers will strike a different zone each time. Your job: choose the right defensive control and deploy it at the right point in the topology — then prove it works.

1 · Arm a controlPick one control from the palette for the wave's attack.
2 · Place itClick the network node where that control belongs.
3 · Run the simulationWatch the attack get blocked, partially mitigated, or breach — with feedback.
⚖️ Mind both metersSecurity Effectiveness and Operational Health. The right control at the right layer wins both.
💡 Before you start
This is applied practice for Lesson 4. The skill being tested is right control, right layer, right place — a control in the wrong zone often fails.
