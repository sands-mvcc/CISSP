🏢 Organizational Profile & High-Stakes Threat Context
Apex Industrial Dynamics (450 Employees)

Apex manufactures precision aerospace turbine alloys and embedded SCADA/OT microcontroller assemblies for prime Department of Defense (DoD) contractors and federal agencies. Under strict CMMC Level 3 / ITAR regulatory oversight, Apex operates under a risk-averse posture where supply chain sabotage or unauthorized firmware tampering directly threatens national defense missions and human safety.

🎯 Measurable Objective

By the end of this lab, you will be able to qualitatively analyze, prioritize, and evaluate risk treatment strategies across complex OT and supply chain threats using impact-likelihood dimensions while balancing live operational cost trade-offs.

💡 CISSP Exam Relevance

CISSP questions consistently test managerial triage over technical reaction. You must recognize that preventing loss of human life and ensuring mission continuity always supersede short-term cost, but technical fixes without senior executive governance and root-cause analysis are exam traps.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Analyze the Briefing: Read the scenario, regulatory boundaries, and OT operational constraints for each round.
  2. Rank & Prioritize: Drag-and-drop or use the [↑] / [↓] arrow controls to order candidate actions/risks from Highest Priority (#1) to Lowest Priority (#4).
  3. Balance the Trade-off Meters: Watch your Projected Risk Reduction and Cost / Operational Friction update live with your chosen prioritization.
  4. Commit Your Rationale: Enter a short managerial justification and confidence level before submitting. (You get 2 attempts per round).
